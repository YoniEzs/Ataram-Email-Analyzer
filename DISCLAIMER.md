# Disclaimer

Ataram Email Analyzer is an experimental heuristic tool for defensive analysis.
It is not antivirus software, a sandbox, a secure email gateway, legal evidence
validation, or a substitute for a trained analyst.

A low score or “no strong indicators detected” verdict is not proof that a
message is safe. A high score is not proof of malicious intent. Forwarding,
mailing lists, broken signatures, incomplete exports and crafted samples can all
produce misleading results.

Do not open, execute or enable content from an attachment because this tool did
not flag it. Analyze only material you are authorized to process and follow the
privacy, evidence-handling and reporting requirements that apply to you.
