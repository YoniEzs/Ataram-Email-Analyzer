ITgalya Email Analyzer

Local-first phishing triage for SOC and DFIR analysts.

v0.1.0-rc1 · Public Beta

The desktop build analyzes the selected email on your workstation, binds only to 127.0.0.1, and does not intentionally visit URLs or execute attachments. Optional DNS/RDAP/reputation enrichment may disclose documented indicators.

EML + MSG

Parses standard MIME email and Outlook message files.

Header forensics

Extracts sender, recipients, timestamps, Reply-To and delivery infrastructure.

URL & attachment triage

Checks suspicious links, homographs, filenames, hashes, archive metadata and YARA indicators.

Analyst reporting

Risk assessment, structured artifacts, JSON export and printable reports.
